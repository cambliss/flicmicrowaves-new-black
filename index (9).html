Home › Services
RF & Microwave Design

Bespoke RF design services

Our experienced engineering team delivers bespoke RF and microwave design services across a broad frequency range from 300 MHz up to 175 GHz. We tackle the most complex challenges in telecommunications, aerospace, defence, and space applications.

Working closely with customers from concept through to full production, we ensure that every design meets the precise technical, environmental, and reliability requirements demanded by mission-critical applications.

Manufacturing

Contract microelectronic manufacturing

Flicmicrowaves' hybrid microelectronic manufacturing capability combines thin-film technology, wire bonding, flip-chip assembly, and precision packaging to produce sub-systems that operate reliably at mmWave frequencies.

Our ISO 9001:2015 certified facility ensures the highest levels of quality assurance throughout the manufacturing process, from raw wafer through to finished product test and verification.

Test & Verification

RF test & verification

Comprehensive RF test and measurement capabilities including vector network analysis, spectrum analysis, and custom automated test equipment for frequencies up to 300 GHz.

Our test engineers work in close collaboration with design teams to ensure every product is validated against specification before delivery, giving customers confidence from day one.

About Flicmicrowaves

Who we are

Flicmicrowaves is a Hyderabad-based designer and manufacturer of advanced RF, microwave and mmWave components and subsystems for critical communication applications.

Home › Company
Our Story

Engineering excellence
from Hyderabad

Founded and based in Hyderabad, Telangana, Flicmicrowaves has built a reputation for delivering world-class RF and microwave engineering solutions to India's most demanding sectors — from defence and aerospace to 5G telecommunications and space exploration.

Our team of expert engineers and technicians brings deep domain expertise in high-frequency electronics design and manufacturing, enabling us to tackle the most complex RF challenges with precision and reliability.
